ABOUT

Building worlds, then rebuilding them better.

I’m a 3D Environment Artist and VRChat Creator based in Scotland. Since 2021, I’ve focused heavily on world building, scene composition and creating spaces that feel atmospheric and comfortable to spend time in.

My portfolio shows both finished environments and the progression behind them. I often revisit similar ideas over time, using each version to improve modelling, lighting, materials, optimisation and overall presentation.

I also create 3D assets, release free and paid work online, and occasionally take freelance commissions.

I completed HNC 3D Computer Animation (SCQF Level 7) in July 2026 and progress to HND 3D Computer Animation (SCQF Level 8) in August 2026.
